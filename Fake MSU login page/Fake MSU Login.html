Gotcha.

You ran a scan. You found an open port. You opened it in a browser.

Then you typed a real username and a real password into a login form you had never seen before, on a host you could not name, over plain HTTP.

Three of those were good instincts. The last one was the lesson.

Nothing was submitted. Nothing was stored. This page has no backend, no database, and no idea what you typed. It is a cardboard cutout of a login screen, and it is running on a Raspberry Pi about twenty feet from you.

An open port is an invitation to look. It was never a reason to trust.
